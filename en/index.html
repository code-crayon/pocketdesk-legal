Pocket Desk Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Last updated: November 27, 2026 (iOS app added)

This policy explains how Code Crayon (코드크레용) ("we") handles information in the Pocket Desk apps for Android and iOS (Android package name and iOS bundle ID com.codecrayon.pocketdesk, including the iOS share extension and Home Screen widget; the "App").

1. Summary

  • The App has no account or sign-in and shows no ads.
  • Files you add, file names and project names are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
  • To improve the App, we collect anonymous usage statistics with Google Firebase Analytics only if you agree. We do not collect the advertising ID (Android advertising ID or iOS IDFA) and we do not track you.

2. Information stored only on your device (not collected by us)

The following stays in the App's private storage (on iOS, an on-device App Group space shared only by the App, its share extension and its widget) and is not sent to us or any third party:

  • Copies of files you share to the App or pick with the system file or photo picker (photos, videos, PDFs, etc.)
  • Project names, icons, colors, layout, lock status and sort settings
  • Copies of photos you choose as a wallpaper or project icon

The App does not request access to all files on your device (Android) or full access to your photo library (iOS). It only receives files you choose or share. On iOS, the share extension copies received files directly into the App's storage on the device, and the Home Screen widget only reads project names, icons and a wallpaper copy stored on the device; nothing is sent off the device.

Project lock asks the operating system to verify your device screen lock (Android: fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, etc.; iOS: Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode). Biometric data is handled by Android or iOS; the App never receives or stores it. The lock hides projects within the App and does not encrypt files.

Device backup

  • Android: the App does not use Android auto backup or device-to-device data transfer. Files and project information stored in the App are not backed up to your Google account or moved to a new device, and they are deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • iOS: files and project information (names, icons, etc.) stored in the App are excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so they are not moved to a new device, and they are deleted when you delete the App. Settings such as your wallpaper choice, desktop layout, taskbar and statistics consent, and a copy of a photo you chose as wallpaper, may be included in your iCloud backup depending on your device's backup settings. Apple keeps that backup in your account; we cannot access it.

3. Information we collect (anonymous usage statistics, only with your consent)

Item Details
App usage Events automatically logged by Firebase Analytics, such as first open, sessions, screen views and app updates
Feature usage How often features are used and whether they succeed or fail (creating/opening projects, adding/viewing/sharing files, search, sort, lock, wallpaper), file count and size ranges, file type (photo/video/PDF/other), splash wait-time range, and usage summaries such as project-count range and whether lock is used
Device and app info Device model, OS version, app version, language, screen size
Identifier App instance ID, a random value Firebase creates per installation (reset when the App is uninstalled). On iOS, collection of the IDFA and IDFV (identifier for vendor) is disabled
Approximate location Country/region-level location derived from IP address (processed by Firebase)

We do not collect: name, email, phone number, advertising ID (including IDFA), precise location, contacts, file contents, file names, project names or search terms.

4. Why we collect it

  • To understand which devices and OS versions use the App, which screens and features are used and where they fail, so we can improve features and find problem environments.
  • We do not use it for advertising, ad personalization, identifying individuals, or sale.
  • We do not combine it with data from other companies' apps or websites to track you, so the iOS app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

5. Retention

  • Files and settings on your device: until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: deleted automatically after 14 months according to our Firebase Analytics data retention setting. Aggregated reports may remain.

6. Third parties and processors

Recipient Data Purpose
Google LLC (Firebase Analytics) Statistics in Section 3 Usage analytics
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Google servers, which may be located outside your country (including the United States).
  • Google's practices: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy , https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • We do not sell or otherwise share personal information with any other third party.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Usage statistics are optional. The App asks for your choice on first launch and collects them only if you agree. All features work without agreeing.
  • You can change your choice at any time: long-press an empty spot on the desktop, open Settings and turn off "Send usage statistics". Collection stops immediately, and unsent statistics and the app instance ID on your device are reset. If you turn it back on, a new ID is used and is not linked to earlier records.
  • Statistics already sent are deleted automatically after the retention period in section 5.
  • If what we collect or why changes, the App will ask for your consent again.
  • You can delete files and projects in the App at any time; uninstalling the App deletes all of them.
  • Uninstalling the App resets the app instance ID, so later statistics are not linked to earlier ones.
  • Statistics are not linked to your name or email, so we generally cannot locate a specific person's data. You may still contact us with questions or deletion requests and we will handle them to the extent we can identify the data.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update and the new effective date on this page.
