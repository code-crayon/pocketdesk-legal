Pocket Desk Support

Pocket Desk (Android and iOS) is a personal file box that keeps files in project folders and lets you save files shared from other apps, then view and share them. There is no account or sign-in, and your files stay on your device.

Note: the app interface is in Korean only.

Contact

  • Email: help@codecrayon.me
  • Helpful details: device model, OS version (e.g. iOS 18.2, Android 15), app version (shown in Settings), and what you were doing when the problem happened
  • You don't need to send your files or personal information.

FAQ

How do I add files from other apps? In Photos, a messenger or a file app, tap Share, choose Pocket Desk, then tap a project. Inside the app, open a folder and use the + button. On iPhone, if Pocket Desk isn't in the share sheet, tap More at the end of the app row and turn it on.

Can I open a project from my Home Screen?

  • iPhone: long-press the Home Screen, add a Pocket Desk widget (small or medium), then edit the widget to choose projects.
  • Android: long-press a project and choose "Add to home screen".

How does project lock work? A locked project opens only after your device's screen lock check (Face ID, Touch ID or passcode on iPhone; fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern on Android). It hides projects inside the app and does not encrypt files. Biometric data is handled by the operating system; the app never receives it.

What happens to my files if I change phones or delete the app? Files stored in the app are not included in backups (iCloud, Google account or computer), so they are not moved to a new device, and deleting the app deletes them. Keep copies of important files elsewhere, for example by selecting them and sharing them.

How do I turn off usage statistics? Long-press an empty spot on the desktop, open Settings and turn off "Send usage statistics". Collection stops and the statistics identifier on your device is reset.

Is the app available in other languages? Currently Korean only.
